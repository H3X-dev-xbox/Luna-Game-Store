## 💎 Beta Access — Paid Early Access

<img src="https://readme-typing-svg.demolab.com?font=Fira+Code&size=15&duration=2500&pause=1000&color=FFB800&center=true&vCenter=true&width=700&lines=Why+is+the+beta+paid%3F;Simple%3A+to+keep+the+servers+alive.;The+full+launch+will+be+100%25+free." alt="Paid Beta" />

</div>

### 🛰️ Why the Beta Is Paid

Running a game launcher isn't free. Every user who downloads a game pulls **massive amounts of bandwidth** from our servers. If we opened the beta to everyone on day one, our infrastructure would collapse under the load — and nobody would get to play anything.

**To keep Luna stable during beta, we're offering a small number of paid early-access slots.** This isn't about profit — it's about survival.

> 🎯 **Every dollar from the beta goes directly to:**
> - 🖥️ **Server hosting** — bandwidth, storage, and uptime
> - 🛡️ **CDN costs** — so downloads are fast for everyone
> - 🔧 **Maintenance** — keeping Luna running smoothly

### 🎁 What Paid Beta Users Get

| Perk | Details |
|---|---|
| 🚀 **Early Access** | Be first to try Luna before public launch |
| 🎮 **Full Catalog** | Access every game in the FitGirl-powered catalog |
| 🐛 **Direct Influence** | Report bugs, suggest features, shape the final product |
| 🏅 **Founder Badge** | A permanent "Beta Founder" tag on your Luna profile |
| 💚 **Lifetime Gratitude** | You helped build Luna. We won't forget that. |

### 🌟 After Beta — Free Forever

**When the beta ends, Luna becomes 100% free for everyone.** No subscriptions, no hidden fees, no paywalls.

Paid beta is a **one-time, limited, temporary** phase. It exists purely to fund the infrastructure that makes the free launch possible. Everyone who joins the beta is directly responsible for bringing Luna to the world.
